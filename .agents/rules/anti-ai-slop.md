# Anti-AI-Slop Writing Rules

Strictly enforce anti-AI-slop writing constraints on all copy, documentation, commit messages, and user-facing text.

## Core Directives
1. **Never use banned buzzwords**: delve, tapestry, landscape, testament, vibrant, pivotal, crucial, intricate, meticulous, bolster, garner, underscore, foster, leverage, utilize, seamless, cutting-edge, game-changing, transformative, revolutionize, robust, comprehensive, empower, elevate, streamline, supercharge.
2. **No Rule of Three**: Break any grouping of three adjectives, three bullet points, or three parallel clauses. Use 2, 4, 1, or 5.
3. **No uniform sentence length**: Mix short punches with longer connective sentences.
4. **No parataxis**: Avoid robotic chains of short repetitive declarations. Use real connective syntax.
5. **No cheerleading or corporate pep talk**: Write with direct technical precision, actual friction, and concrete operational facts.
6. **No AI openers**: Never start sentences with "Certainly", "Furthermore", "Moreover", "In today's", "It's worth noting", "At its core".
7. **Punctuation discipline**: Maximum 1 em dash per 500 words. Maximum 1 exclamation mark per 1,000 words.
